About

I build the systems agencies will run on.

I’m Damon Henry, founder and CEO of KORTX. Since 2014 we’ve worked with more than 350 agencies and managed more than $300 million in media. KORTX works as an agency API: a layer agencies plug into for measurement, operations and AI, instead of building it all themselves.

I’m an engineer first. I still build the systems, and this site is where I write down what I learn doing it: what works, what doesn’t, and what I got wrong. No hype. Every essay is a draft chapter of a book, Engineering the Modern Agency.

I didn’t take the straight path here. I got kicked out of public school, and a basketball coach taught me that life is mostly what you make of it. That story is here.

I’m also an angel investor. Two of the companies I’ve been fortunate to be involved with have been acquired. For the full résumé, see LinkedIn.

Away from work I’m a husband and a dad of two, which mostly means sitting in the stands at my kids’ games. When I get time of my own, it goes to golf, the boat or a snowboard.
