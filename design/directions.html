Every platform grades its own homework

Platforms now automate the buying. Proving what worked is still up to you.

DH
Damon Henry · 9 min read · Watch the 6-min video

Last month one of our agency partners had 1,000 orders. Their platforms reported 1,340. Nobody lied. Each platform counted by its own rules, and all three claimed credit for many of the same customers.

This isn’t a tracking bug you can fix. It’s how attribution works. And more and more, it’s how your client’s budget gets decided.

Attribution shows what customers touched, not what media caused.

The fix isn’t a better dashboard. It’s changing the question you answer for your client.

INSTEAD OF“Facebook had a 2.5x ROAS.” SAY

“Our geo test showed Meta drove $120K in incremental revenue at a CAC 20% below target. We recommend a 15% budget increase.”

For the technical reader: reconciling claims in 6 lines of SQL 
-- how much are platforms over-claiming?
SELECT month,
       SUM(platform_conversions)          AS claimed,
       MAX(store_orders)                  AS actual,
       SUM(platform_conversions) / MAX(store_orders) AS overclaim
FROM monthly_channel_summary GROUP BY month;

What to do Monday

  1. Pull last month’s platform-reported conversions and add them up.
  2. Compare the total with actual orders from the client’s store or CRM.
  3. Show that one number to your client. It opens the incrementality conversation.
